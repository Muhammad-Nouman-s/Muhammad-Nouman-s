### Muhammad Nouman

I am a PhD researcher in Autonomous Systems at the Telematics Lab (DEI), Politecnico di Bari, Italy, affiliated with CNIT. I work on reliable sensing, localization and security for connected autonomous systems.

My research focuses on:

- Bluetooth 6.0 and BLE localization: advertising, channel sounding, presence and motion-state estimation from BLE logs
- Machine learning for anomaly and attack detection in IoT and wireless sensor networks

Repositories:

- [Bluetoothctl-MQTT-Device-Analytics](https://github.com/Muhammad-Nouman-s/Bluetoothctl-MQTT-Device-Analytics): offline analysis of BLE scan data collected over MQTT (presence sessions, iBeacon identifiers, RSSI proximity and movement)
- [Artificial-intelligence-and-Machine-Learning](https://github.com/Muhammad-Nouman-s/Artificial-intelligence-and-Machine-Learning): KNN hyperparameter study for IoT intrusion detection on the TON_IoT dataset

Tools I use: Python, PyTorch, scikit-learn, pandas, Jupyter, MQTT, Linux/BlueZ, LaTeX.
