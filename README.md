## Muhammad Nouman

PhD researcher in Autonomous Systems at the Telematics Lab (DEI), Politecnico di Bari, Italy, affiliated with CNIT.

I work on reliable sensing, localization and security for connected autonomous systems. My research combines Bluetooth 6.0/BLE localization with machine learning for anomaly and attack detection in IoT networks.

### Research

- **Bluetooth 6.0 and BLE localization**: advertising, channel sounding, presence and motion-state estimation from irregular BLE advertisement logs, and throughput optimization for BLE extended advertising.
- **Learning-based anomaly and attack detection**: machine learning for detecting attacks and malicious nodes in IoT and wireless sensor networks, including boosting-based detectors and blockchain-backed data storage.
